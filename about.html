About the evidence

Hand Pain Explorer is an educational tool. It shows what lies under the skin where your hand hurts, what commonly causes pain there, and what usually helps. It is not a diagnosis and it cannot examine you.

Review status: . The content was checked against the published sources listed below. It has not yet been reviewed by a licensed clinician. If you are one and can help, see REVIEWERS.md.

What it does and doesn't claim

  • It explains anatomy and lists common causes of pain in 17 areas of the hand, wrist and forearm, with plain-language tips and warning signs.
  • "What might fit" ranks those common causes by how well they match what you entered. It does not calculate probabilities and it has not been validated on real patients.
  • It cannot detect anything it hasn't been told, cannot feel, see or image your hand, and does not know your history or medications.

How the ranking works

Each saved spot contributes to every condition listed for that area. The score is a sum of simple weights, all of which live in js/content.js and js/analysis.js where a reviewer can read and change them:

InputEffect
A spot in an areaEach cause listed for that area gets a base score, scaled by pain (0.35 at 0/10 to 1.0 at 10/10) and ×1.4 if the cause is marked common there.
A symptom that matches a cause's tags+0.9 per match (×pain scale). "Red, hot or fever", "Cut, bite or puncture" and "Can't bend or straighten it" count double, because they separate emergencies from sprains.
NumbnessEach numb spot is matched to the skin nerve that supplies it (median, ulnar or radial, drawn by the nerve map). +1.4 per spot inside a condition's nerve area; −0.5 per spot outside it. Numb palm skin counts against carpal tunnel syndrome because that skin branch bypasses the tunnel. Numbness on the back of the hand favours the elbow over the wrist for the ulnar nerve. Numbness across two or more nerve areas raises "nerve irritation from the neck or a general nerve problem".
How longHours–days: +0.6 to injury-type causes, −0.5 to wear-and-tear causes. Months: the reverse.
Both hands+0.7 to conditions that affect the whole body (rheumatoid and psoriatic arthritis, osteoarthritis, Raynaud's, carpal tunnel, Dupuytren's, basal thumb arthritis).
Self-checksEach test has its own weight reflecting how informative it is (for example Phalen's +1.8 for yes, −0.6 for no; Tinel's +1.5 / −0.4). The reliability note on each card quotes the published sensitivity and specificity where known.
Several spots+0.6 for each extra spot a condition explains.

The label on each result depends on how much evidence there is, not only on rank. With a location and nothing else, every cause is "Common here". "Most consistent" needs at least two independent pieces of evidence. Urgent conditions whose key symptom you ticked are shown in a separate "Don't miss" list regardless of rank, and the red-flag checklist comes before any ranking.

Who checked it

The content was written and then checked, condition by condition, against the sources below by the author with AI assistance. Every link was opened and its title confirmed. The full method, the corrections it produced and the known limitations are in CONTENT_REVIEW.md. Reported corrections are tracked as GitHub issues.

Sources

Patient information from the NHS and the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (OrthoInfo), clinical summaries from StatPearls (NCBI Bookshelf) and a few PubMed Central reviews. The app paraphrases these sources in plain language; it does not reproduce their text. NHS content is published under the Open Government Licence; StatPearls chapters are published under a Creative Commons Attribution licence; OrthoInfo and PMC articles are cited as references only.

Privacy

  • Your pain map, answers and settings are stored only in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server. Use Export to keep a copy.
  • Webcam video is processed on your device by MediaPipe and never leaves it.
  • Loading the page fetches: the 3D library and hand-tracking code from cdn.jsdelivr.net, the hand-tracking model from storage.googleapis.com, and fonts from fonts.googleapis.com. These requests carry no information about you beyond the ordinary web request. There are no analytics and no cookies.
