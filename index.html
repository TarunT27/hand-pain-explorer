    How it works

    • Explore: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, hover to name any structure, click a spot to see possible causes of pain there.
    • Peel: drag the Layers slider (or press [ ]) to dissolve skin, then muscles, tendons and nerves down to the bones.
    • Pain map: save each sore spot with a 0–10 pain level and what it feels like. What might fit combines your spots, numbness pattern and self-check answers into a ranked list you can copy for a clinician. Saved only in this browser.
    • Webcam: hold one hand up and the 3D hand copies your pose and rotation. Point at a sore spot on that hand with your other index finger and hold still to open it. Pinch with both hands and pull apart to peel.
    • Movements: play gentle exercises on the model — tendon glides, nerve glides, thumb circles and more.

    Video is processed on your device by MediaPipe and never uploaded.

    Sources & review

    This is an educational tool, not medical advice. It can't diagnose you. If pain is severe, follows an injury, lasts more than a couple of weeks, or comes with numbness, weakness, fever or colour change, see a clinician.
