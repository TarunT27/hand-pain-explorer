    How it works

    • Explore: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, hover (or tap) to name any structure, click a spot to see possible causes of pain there. Keyboard: focus the model, then arrow keys rotate, + − zoom, R resets.
    • See inside: drag the slider (or press [ ]) to peel skin, then muscles, tendons and nerves down to the bones.
    • My pain map: save each sore spot with a 0–10 pain level, how long it has hurt and what it feels like. What might fit combines your spots, numbness pattern, red-flag answers and self-check answers into a ranked list you can print or copy for a clinician. Saved only in this browser; export it as a file to keep it.
    • Webcam: hold one hand up and the 3D hand copies your pose and rotation. Point at a sore spot on that hand with your other index finger and hold still to open it. Pinch with both hands and pull apart to peel.
    • Movements: play gentle movements on the model — tendon glides, nerve glides, thumb circles and more. They are withheld for spots you've marked as injured.

    Video is processed on your device by MediaPipe and never uploaded. There are no analytics.

    Sources & review

    This is an educational tool, not medical advice. It can't diagnose you. If pain is severe, follows an injury, lasts more than a couple of weeks, or comes with numbness, weakness, fever or colour change, see a clinician.
